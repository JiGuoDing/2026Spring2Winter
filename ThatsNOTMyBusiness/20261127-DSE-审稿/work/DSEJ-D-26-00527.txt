Abstract:
Entrepreneurship education is crucial in nurturing innovative thinking, ability to think
and solve problems as well as entrepreneurial intentions in university students.
However, the traditional forms of teaching are not typically customized and they fail to
address the different student learning needs. To bridge this gap, this paper introduces
Entrepreneurship Education White Shark Optimization using Intelligent Learning
Support Vector Machine (EEWSO-ILSVM) model to build individualized support system
under the guidance of intelligent learning paths. A structured questionnaire survey was
used to obtain a dataset of 330 valid responses of students in various fields, covering
accounting, computer applications, machine operations and preschool education. The
questions about the Internet use, on-line studying, and expectations to receive
personal assistance were asked to the students and the entrepreneurial skills were
rated on the six-item scale with the focus on the entrepreneurial abilities, problem-
solving, and intentions. The suggested EEWSO-ILSVM combines the optimization
ability of the White Shark Optimization algorithm with the predictive ability of an SVM,
which is able to adequately represent tendencies in the learning trajectory and
entrepreneurial development of students. Comparative experiments against the
existing models include SVM, GA-SVM, LSTM and CNN-LSTM, which prove that
EEWSO-ILSVM performs better in terms of accuracy as 92.1%, precision as 91%,
recall as 90.2%, F1-score as 90.6% and AUC-ROC as highest performance. Moreover,
radar and bar chart analyses indicate that with this model students show great
improvements in their entrepreneurial competencies. The results indicate that support
systems based on intelligent learning trajectory have the potential to improve
entrepreneurship education by allowing personalized intervention, limiting
misclassification in competency evaluation, and promoting entrepreneurial
preparedness in students.

Abstract 
Entrepreneurship education is crucial in nurturing innovative thinking, ability to think and solve problems 
as well as entrepreneurial intentions in university students. However, the traditional forms of teaching are 
not typically customized and they fail to address the different student learning needs. To bridge this gap, 
this paper introduces Entrepreneurship Education White Shark Optimization using Intelligent Learning 
Support Vector Machine (EEWSO-ILSVM) model to build individualized support system under the 
guidance of intelligent learning paths. A structured questionnaire survey was used to obtain a dataset of 330 
valid responses of students in various fields, covering accounting, computer applications, machine 
operations and preschool education. The questions about the Internet use, on-line studying, and expectations 
to receive personal assistance were asked to the students and the entrepreneurial skills were rated on the 
six-item scale with the focus on the entrepreneurial abilities, problem-solving, and intentions. The 
suggested EEWSO-ILSVM combines the optimization ability of the White Shark Optimization algorithm 
with the predictive ability of an SVM, which is able to adequately represent tendencies in the learning 
trajectory and entrepreneurial development of students. Comparative experiments against the existing 
models include SVM, GA-SVM, LSTM and CNN-LSTM, which prove that EEWSO-ILSVM performs 
better in terms of accuracy as 92.1%, precision as 91%, recall as 90.2%, F1-score as 90.6% and AUC-ROC 
as highest performance. Moreover, radar and bar chart analyses indicate that with this model students show 
great improvements in their entrepreneurial competencies. The results indicate that support systems based 
on intelligent learning trajectory have the potential to improve entrepreneurship education by allowing 
personalized intervention, limiting misclassification in competency evaluation, and promoting 
entrepreneurial preparedness in students. 
Keywords: Entrepreneurship education, intelligent learning, learning trajectories, EEWSO-ILSVM.

1.1 Research Objective 
The purpose of the study is to develop a personalized support system of university entrepreneurship 
education through the analysis of intelligent learning processes and application of the EEWSO-ILSVM 
model to the task of precise assessment and improvement of entrepreneurial competencies, problem-solving 
skills, and entrepreneurial intentions. 
1.2 Contribution of the Study 
The contributions made in this study to the body of entrepreneurship education include the construction of 
the EEWSO-ILSVM model which involves the use of White Shark Optimization plus Support Vector 
Machines to build an individualized support system using intelligent learning paths. One of the key 
advantages of the proposed method to address individual differences in learning behaviors and 
entrepreneurial competencies is the ability to provide students with a personalized approach, unlike 
traditional methods, which assume that the groups are homogeneous. On the basis of this model, using the 
data obtained in this study through secondary technical schools, the study illustrates how a machine learning 
algorithm that optimizes its results can be used to predict accurately and optimize entrepreneurial potential 
and problem-solving capacity as well as entrepreneurial intentions. The addition of full performance 
analysis indicators (accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and AUC-ROC) confirm the superiority of the 
suggested model in comparison with traditional SVM, GA-SVM, LSTM, and CNN-LSTM. Additionally, 
the use of visual analytics such as radar charts, bar graphs and confusion matrices provide quantifiable data 
to teachers, policy makers, and education institutions. In sum, the current study presents a new 
methodological framework and empirical data demonstrating that smart, data-driven models can reshape 
the entrepreneurship education process to create individualized learning backgrounds and improve the 
entrepreneurial preparedness of university students.

3.3 Feature Extraction Using Principal Component Analysis (PCA) 
Prior to being fed into the EEWSO-ILSVM model, multivariate learning data is optimized using PCA, an 
effective dimensionality reduction technique. Because direct processing of raw data might result in higher 
computing burden and decreased model generalization, this strategy lowers dimensionality and redundancy 
in the system. By converting a big collection of possibly correlated variables into principle components, 
PCA preserves the majority of the information in the original dataset and guarantees effective, scalable, and 
quicker learning in the EEWSO-ILSVM-based hazard prediction. The following stages are used to illustrate 
the PCA method.

5. Conclusion 
This work proves that the suggested Entrepreneurship Education White Shark Optimization on the basis of 
Intelligent Learning Support Vector Machine (EEWSO-ILSVM) model offers a strong and efficient 
application of the support system building in university entrepreneurship education. Through the 
combination of smart learning paths and cutting-edge optimization and classification algorithms, one would 
be able to assess the entrepreneurial capabilities of the learners, such as skills, aptitudes, problem-solving, 
and entrepreneurship intention, with high accuracy. The analysis of EEWSO-ILSVM against the traditional 
models like SVM, GA-SVM, LSTM, and CNN-LSTM has clearly revealed that EEWSO-ILSVM performs 
better in various measurement parameters, including accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and AUC-ROC. 
Furthermore, it is proved that the model has the potential to reduce misclassification and maximize custom 
learning interventions through the confusion matrix analysis. Moreover, the radar and bar chart presentation 
proves that the growth of entrepreneurial abilities has improved significantly, and it is possible to say that 
the model positively affects the learning results of students. Overall, the paper presents an innovative 
approach that can be adopted to supplement intelligent optimization and entrepreneurship education that 
provides future entrepreneurs with a data-driven, high-quality, and personalized learning process. 
Future Enhancement 
Although the further evolution of the proposed EEWSO-ILSVM model may be generalized to any other 
sphere, the further research activities may be performed in a great number of ways, to make the given model 
more feasible and applicable. First, the real-time use of digital platform learning analytics may allow the 
further refinement of learning pathways tracking, which can offer personalized support that changes 
dynamically. Second, to increase the generalizability of the model and the power of the model, the model 
needs to be generalized to other universities and other disciplines. Third, the analysis of multimodal data, 
including behavioral records, social, and psychological indicators, may lead to more insights into the 
entrepreneurial preparedness than survey-based measures. We can also add that future systems can 
implement hybrid deep learning models that use reinforcement learning to constantly optimize custom 
interventions. And finally, to convert intelligence into a practical tool to be utilized by students, teachers, 
and administrators to establish a complete entrepreneurship education ecosystem, the intelligence of the 
model can be utilized to create an interactive dashboard or a mobile-based support platform.
