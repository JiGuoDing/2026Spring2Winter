Abstract:
The behavior of innovation and entrepreneurship is a key factor in the development of
the enterprise and it is difficult to determine the pattern of behavior and predict the
success of an entrepreneur because of multidimensional circumstances. In this
research, a data mining methodology is offered that models and classifies
entrepreneurial behavior by using K-Means clustering and Enhanced Naive Bayes
(ENB) classification. The survey was designed as a structured survey of 136
businesses in China, which entailed the data on the adoption of digital marketing, size
of the enterprise, and the management features. K-Means classification grouped
enterprises into three categories with meanings of Digitally Mature Innovators,
Moderate Adopters and Low Adopters demonstrating some specific patterns of
innovation and digital involvement. ENB was then used to categorize types of
entrepreneurial behavior, such as Innovative, Risk-taking, Proactive and Conservative
behaviors. ENB was compared to common classifiers, including Naïve Bayes, Decision
Trees, Logistic Regression, Random Forest and SVM. Findings suggest that the
suggested ENB model demonstrated the best classification performance with an
accuracy of 94.6, precision of 93.8, recall of 94.2, and F1-score of 94.0 and better than
all baseline approaches. Analysis of confusion matrices revealed that there were small
misclassifications and both ROC and PR-AUC curves indicated a strong predictive
ability in all the behavioral classes. The results emphasize the usefulness of combining
clustering and refined classification to reveal patterns of entrepreneurial behavior to
generate practical recommendations to enterprise managers and policy makers. This
research has an addition to the methodological development of the entrepreneurship
study considering that it provides a credible framework of the behavioral modeling,
classification, and performance appraisal.

Modeling and Path Analysis of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Behavior Based on Data

Abstract 
The behavior of innovation and entrepreneurship is a key factor in the development of the 
enterprise and it is difficult to determine the pattern of behavior and predict the success of an 
entrepreneur because of multidimensional circumstances. In this research, a data mining 
methodology is offered that models and classifies entrepreneurial behavior by using K-Means 
clustering and Enhanced Naive Bayes (ENB) classification. The survey was designed as a 
structured survey of 136 businesses in China, which entailed the data on the adoption of digital 
marketing, size of the enterprise, and the management features. K-Means classification grouped 
enterprises into three categories with meanings of Digitally Mature Innovators, Moderate 
Adopters and Low Adopters demonstrating some specific patterns of innovation and digital 
involvement. ENB was then used to categorize types of entrepreneurial behavior, such as 
Innovative, Risk-taking, Proactive and Conservative behaviors. ENB was compared to common 
classifiers, including Naïve Bayes, Decision Trees, Logistic Regression, Random Forest and 
SVM. Findings suggest that the suggested ENB model demonstrated the best classification 
performance with an accuracy of 94.6, precision of 93.8, recall of 94.2, and F1-score of 94.0 and 
better than all baseline approaches. Analysis of confusion matrices revealed that there were small 
misclassifications and both ROC and PR-AUC curves indicated a strong predictive ability in all 
the behavioral classes. The results emphasize the usefulness of combining clustering and refined 
classification to reveal patterns of entrepreneurial behavior to generate practical 
recommendations to enterprise managers and policy makers. This research has an addition to the 
methodological development of the entrepreneurship study considering that it provides a credible 
framework of the behavioral modeling, classification, and performance appraisal. 
Keywords: Data mining, path analysis, entrepreneurship behavior, K-means clustering, 
Enhanced naïve Bayes classification. 
I. 
Introduction 
The advent of new digital dynamics has changed the way businesses function and expand in 
recent years, drastically changing the entrepreneurial and business development scene.  The 
proliferation of digital technologies has opened up a world of possibilities for entrepreneurs and 
reduced the hurdles to entry for new firms [1].  Previously only huge, established organisations 
could compete on a global stage, develop swiftly, and scale efficiently with the use of digital 
platforms and tools.  Entrepreneurship has become more accessible to more people as a result of 
the widespread use of digital technologies in company operations.  Platforms on digital provide 
minimum-cost entry channels for beginners and small firms, in contrast to traditional business 
models that necessitated substantial initial financial investment and physical presence.  Online e-
commerce, social networking, and digital marketing have made it easier than ever for 
entrepreneurs to start and run their enterprises online [2].  There has been an upsurge in 
entrepreneurial activity as a result of this accessibility, since more people are able to launch and 
expand their own firms without being limited by location or having a large financial backing.

 Capturing data and leveraging technology to generate value is a key driver of innovation in the
